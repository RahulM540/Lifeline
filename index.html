Lifeline Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Lifeline is a student project that helps you find and cancel subscriptions you no longer want. It does this by reading billing emails in your Gmail inbox, listing the subscriptions it finds, and asking for your approval before any action is taken. This policy explains what data Lifeline accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a hashed (bcrypt) version of your password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Gmail data (only if you connect Gmail): Lifeline requests the read-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly). It searches your inbox for billing receipts and promotional offers from services you use.
  • Information extracted from those emails: service name, plan, amount, currency, billing cycle, email date, next billing date, and, for offers, a title, short summary and link. We also store the Gmail message ID so the same email is not processed twice.
  • Activity in the app: your approve/deny decisions, action logs, and your chat messages with the Lifeline assistant.
  • Gmail access token: the token Google gives Lifeline so it can keep reading your inbox until you disconnect.

2. How we use your information

We use the data only to provide Lifeline's features: detecting subscriptions and offers, showing them in your dashboard, answering your questions in chat, and carrying out cancellations you approve. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. How Gmail data is processed

  • Lifeline only reads email. It cannot send, delete, or change anything in your mailbox.
  • Email text is analysed by an AI model that runs locally on the Lifeline server (Ollama). Your email content is not sent to third-party AI services.
  • Full email bodies are not saved in our database. Only the extracted details listed in section 1 are stored.
  • No humans read your emails, except where needed for security, to comply with the law, or with your explicit consent.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Lifeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Gmail is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above, and is not used for advertising or to train generalised AI models.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data or Gmail data with third parties. We may disclose information only if required by law.

6. Storage and security

Data is stored in a database and token files on the Lifeline server. Passwords are hashed, sessions use signed tokens, and access to your data requires you to be logged in. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect Gmail: go to Settings → Gmail → Disconnect. Lifeline deletes its stored token and stops reading your inbox.
  • Delete your account: go to Settings and delete your account. This permanently removes your account and the data associated with it.
  • Revoke access at Google: you can remove Lifeline at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Children

Lifeline is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
